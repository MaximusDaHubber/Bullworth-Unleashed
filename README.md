# Bullworth Unleashed

A huge all-in-one mod for **Bully: Scholarship Edition** (PC) with an **18-page in-game mod menu**: superpowers, zombie mode, a spawner with the boss-fight versions of every boss, Hitman contracts, a GTA wanted system, shop heists, Boss Rush, Gang Wars with a live turf map, Halloween night, **Fight Club**, **carjacking**, **mission replay** with a cutscene viewer, **249 playable characters** with their own fighting styles, a music selector and more.

## New in v1.3

- **Fight Club** - a tournament in Russell's pit with betting, and endless fights in 14 arenas with 9 difficulties, boss rounds, a shop, allies, gambling, stocks and a robbery
- **Carjacking** - drag drivers out of their cars (Y / F). Witnesses mean wanted stars; police cars get sirens (B / Space)
- **Cutscene viewer** - 108 story cutscenes, unlocked as you finish their missions (Mission Replay page)
- **Boss Rush: all bosses at once** - all 7 bosses together, with their own best time
- **Clique respect** - +5 / -5 per clique, and the cliques act on it (Cheats)
- **New cheats** - all hobo moves, all gym grapple moves, all classes passed, all clothing items
- **Menu** - Bullworth colors and the game's own menu sounds
- **Fixes** - Play as (Darby, getting busted, the girls' dorm as a girl) and the wanted system

Ideas for clique respect, Boss Rush all at once, the menu sounds and colors and the cutscene viewer by **ussi11**.

## New in v1.2

- **Mission Replay** - replay any story mission you've finished, chapter by chapter. The chapter can't change, everything is put back afterwards, chapter 3 is snowy, Halloween is at night, and missions that lead into each other in the story still do
- **Play as: 249 characters** - every clique, the girls, students, teachers, town people and everyone's other outfits, each with their own fighting style (boss moves for the bosses, Norton's sledgehammer, Bif's boxing, the girls' slaps and kicks), or pick any of 30 styles yourself
- **Music selector** - 234 of the game's songs (World > Music)
- **Cheats** - God mode moved here, **Never get wanted**, **Kiss everyone** (teenage students only)
- **Hairstyles** moved to Outfits

## New in v1.1 - the Halloween update

- **Halloween Night** - always night, the town decorated for Halloween and only kids in costume on the streets
- **6 Halloween costumes** - a new Zombie costume, a Creepy clown with clown makeup, a Devil with a red face, Pumpkin head, Alien and a Werewolf mask
- **Zombie Skins on/off switch** - two .bat files turn the zombie look on and off (off by default)

## Requirements

- Bully: Scholarship Edition (PC)
- **Derpy's Script Loader (DSL) 16 or newer** - install this first
- Recommended: **SilentPatch for Bully** (not required)

## Install

1. Close Bully.
2. Extract the zip into your Bully folder (the one with `Bully.exe`). Nothing of the game is replaced.
3. Run `BullworthUnleashed\Install new outfits.bat` once to make the Hitman suit and the Halloween costumes wearable (updating from 1.0? run it too - it only adds what's missing).
4. Optional: run `BullworthUnleashed\Zombie skins ON.bat` for the full zombie look (`Zombie skins OFF.bat` switches it back).
5. Start the game and open the menu: **M** on keyboard, **hold LT + Look Back** on controller.

The full controls and how every mode works are in `BullworthUnleashed\README.txt` inside the zip.

## What's included

**Sandbox tools**
- **Superpowers** - flight, super jump, super strength, god mode, slow motion, fire shockwave, telekinesis
- **Cheats** - god mode, never get wanted, infinite ammo, kiss everyone, all weapons, money, carnival tickets, freeze time, all collectibles
- **Teleport** - Rockstar's own debug teleport list
- **World** - music selector, time, weather (snow and Christmas snow too), holiday decorations
- **Play as** - 249 characters, each with their own fighting style, or pick any of 30 styles
- **Outfits** - the game's special outfits, the new Hitman suit, 6 Halloween costumes, hairstyles and the **wardrobe anywhere**
- **Vehicles** - bikes, karts, cars and the 3D arcade ships, plus carjacking
- **Spawner** - friends, enemies, group fights and the boss-fight versions of the story bosses

**Game modes**
- **Arcade** - Rockstar's hidden full 3D version of the arcade race
- **GTA Wanted System** - 1 to 5 stars with prefects, cops, orderlies and the Principal
- **Zombie Mode** - waves of zombies at night with zombie bosses (switch on Zombie Skins for the full zombie look)
- **Hitman: Bullworth** - contracts, disguises and Silent Assassin ratings
- **Heists** - hold up shops, then escape the cops to keep the cash
- **Boss Rush** - all 7 story bosses back to back in Russell's pit
- **Gang Wars** - join a clique and take Bullworth district by district
- **Halloween Night** - always night, Halloween decorations and costume kids everywhere
- **Mission Replay** - replay any story mission you've finished, with safeguards, and the cutscene viewer
- **Fight Club** - tournaments with betting and endless fights in 14 arenas
